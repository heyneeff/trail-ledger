# The Trail Ledger

A cursed Oregon Trail clone, framed as a mortician-kept expedition ledger — green phosphor CRT terminal, a numbered menu like the original, and a supernatural curse laid over the top of the usual dysentery-and-broken-axles simulation.

Woven in alongside the absurdist stuff is the real history the original game glosses over: the land wasn't empty, the Army's bison extermination was deliberate policy, the Whitman Mission killings and reprisal actually happened, and the Willamette Valley was Kalapuya land before it was anyone's happy ending. Those beats are kept factual and separate from the jokes — no punchlines attached to real history.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no dependencies, nothing to install.

## What's in it

- Outfit at the Hollow, name your five-person party, pick a trade (Mortician / Carny / Cultist)
- Travel the numbered menu: continue on the trail, check supplies, check the party, look at the map, change pace/rations, hunt
- River crossings, forts, random cursed events, illness and death with an epitaph generator
- A canvas hunting minigame
- Scored ending with a rank
